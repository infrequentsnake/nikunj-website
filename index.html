Latest project

Buying House Calculator

Can you actually afford a home in Sydney?

A bank can tell you what you are allowed to borrow in about four minutes. It cannot tell you whether you will still have money at 75. That gap is the whole Sydney housing conversation, and almost nobody models it.

So I built the thing that does. Two paths, month by month, for any age between 18 and 75. Same income, same groceries, same returns, same retirement age. The only things that change are when you buy and whether you rent while you wait.

About

"Numbers do not feel. Do not bleed or weep or hope." — Amie Kaufman.

Actuarial work appeals to me because it refuses to let a question stay vague. How much should this cost? How likely is it, really? What happens if I’m wrong by ten percent? Those questions have answers, and my goal is to acknowledge and master the tools that are required to find them.

In my free time, I absolutely love playing tennis; I have had a racquet in my hand since as long as I can remember. Tennis really brings about resilience in me, for it is a gruesome sport, some days feel effortless whereas others an uphill battle. It teaches you that progress in non-linear and building confidence in your own ability is the biggest factor out of anything else. I aim to apply this in the game of Life.

I hope some of my projects provide genuine insight into others. For any questions at all, reach out!
