About

Numbers, and the decisions that rest on them.

Actuarial work appeals to me because it refuses to let a question stay vague. How much should this cost? How likely is it, really? What happens if we’re wrong by ten percent? Those questions have answers, and the discipline of finding them well is what drew me to the degree.

At UNSW I’m building the foundations — financial mathematics, probability, statistics and the modelling tools that turn a messy dataset into something a business can actually act on. The projects on this site are what I do with those tools in my own time, away from the syllabus, where the questions are messier and nobody sets the brief.

Outside of study, I’m putting together a set of charity projects that use the same analytical toolkit for causes I care about. That work is in progress and will live here when it’s ready.
